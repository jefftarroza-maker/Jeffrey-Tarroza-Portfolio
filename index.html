Instructional Design

Sample IB Science Lesson Plan

A showcase of inquiry-based lesson design aligned to IB Learner Profile and 5E Learning Framework.

Grade 6 Science

Matter: Plant Parts and Their Functions

IB Inquiry Framework

IB Learner Profile Integration:

  • 🔍 Inquirer: Ask questions and investigate plant structures.
  • 💡 Thinker: Analyze how plant parts work together.
  • 🌱 Caring: Appreciate the importance of plants in sustaining life.

Provocative Inquiry Question:

"What would happen if a plant lost one of its parts? Could it still survive? Why or why not?"

5E Inquiry Learning Activities:

1. Engage

Observe real or virtual plant models and predict functions.

2. Explore

In pairs, label plant structures and discuss adaptations.

3. Explain

Share scientific findings and compare ideas with peers.

4. Extend

Design a 'super plant' modified for extreme environments.

5. Reflect

Write one new understanding and one remaining question.
